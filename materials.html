Materials

What goes into every sheet.

We choose fibre by product, not by habit — balancing softness, strength and environmental footprint for facial tissue, rolls, towels and wipes alike.

Virgin wood pulp

Long-fibre softwood pulp sourced from FSC-aligned plantations, used where extra strength and softness are needed, such as facial tissue and pocket packs.

Recycled fibre

Post-consumer and pre-consumer waste paper, de-inked and re-pulped, forming the base of our standard toilet paper and kitchen towel lines.

Bamboo fibre

A fast-renewing, lower-water-footprint fibre blended into select toilet paper and napkin ranges for a firmer sheet.

Non-woven spunlace fabric

A cotton-viscose blend substrate used for wet wipes, chosen for its wet strength and compatibility with our cleansing solution.

Sourcing & sustainability commitments

Closed-loop water recycling across our pulping and forming lines.

Chlorine-free (ECF) bleaching across our pulp-based product lines.

Reduced-plastic and recyclable packaging across the range.

Traceable fibre sourcing, verified against supplier documentation.

From fibre to finished sheet

1

Fibre sourcing

Pulp, recycled stock and non-woven fabric are received and matched against incoming-material specifications.

2

Pulping & refining

Fibres are pulped, cleaned and refined to the length and freeness needed for each product.

3

Sheet forming & creping

Pulp is formed into a continuous web, dried and creped to build softness and bulk.

4

Converting & folding

The base sheet is embossed, plied, cut and folded into its finished format — roll, box or pack.

5

Packaging & dispatch

Finished goods are packed, batch-coded and staged for dispatch to distributors and retailers.
